# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Be considerate and professional.
- Give actionable, technical feedback.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or abusive language.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments and contributions that violate this code of conduct.
